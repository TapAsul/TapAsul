Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TapAsul technology and ordering.

How does the TapAsul Digital Card work?

TapAsul uses embedded NFC (Near Field Communication) chips. When tapped against any modern iOS or Android smartphone, it instantly opens your custom dynamic landing page—allowing clients to save your contact info or view links without installing any app.

What is the ₱149 monthly hosting fee for?

The ₱149 monthly subscription maintains your dynamic web page server, analytics tracking, contact vCard download links, and allows you to request real-time updates to your contact details anytime.

How do customers leave a review on the Acrylic Standee?

Customers simply tap their smartphone against the top of the acrylic standee (or scan the high-clarity QR code). It instantly redirects them directly to your Google Business Review form or social media page.
